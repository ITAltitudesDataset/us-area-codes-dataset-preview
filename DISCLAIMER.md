This dataset is a static telecommunications and geographic reference snapshot intended for research, educational analysis, business intelligence, and non-production testing.

## 2. Data Currency

Area code assignments, geographic coverage, and numbering statuses may change after the snapshot date.

Users should verify current information against authoritative sources before making operational decisions.

## 3. Geographic Limitations

- Area codes do not establish a subscriber's exact physical location.
- Some area codes may serve multiple geographic regions.
- Certain area codes are non-geographic, reserved, or planned.
- City, state, county, and time-zone mappings may be approximate or incomplete.
